Fix this today

Your weekly staff schedules are public on the internet.

Six schedule PDFs are sitting in your website's media library where anyone can download them, no password. We opened the week of August 17 to 23 and it lists roughly 55 employees by name against their shifts. Front of house and back of house, both. A new one goes up every week, so this is how somebody on your team is sharing the schedule, not a one-time slip.

You do not need us to fix it. Delete the files, and post the schedule somewhere that asks for a login. We are telling you first because it is the kind of thing an owner should hear from a stranger before it becomes a problem.

Reservations are a request, not a booking.

The part that matters most

Your website collects requests. It should complete transactions.

Every path on your site right now ends with a person on your payroll doing something by hand. A reservation is a form somebody answers. A gift card is an envelope somebody addresses. A carryout order is a phone call somebody keys in. A banquet inquiry is an email somebody chases. The Marshall Historical Society sells Home Tour tickets online through Square. A volunteer group can take money on the internet, and the best restaurant in town cannot.
